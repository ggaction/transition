# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful and specific in issues, reviews, and discussions. Critique code and behavior, not personal identity or competence. Help newcomers, preserve attribution, and respect privacy and other people's time.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, publishing private information without permission, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome when it stays focused on evidence and the task.

Report concerns privately to Hyeon Jeon at [jeonhyun97@gmail.com](mailto:jeonhyun97@gmail.com). Include relevant context and any immediate safety concern. If the concern involves that contact, GitHub's platform reporting tools are an alternative. Reports are handled as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request changes in behavior, pause discussions, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Responses should reflect impact and repeated behavior. Where appropriate, explain the decision and consider relevant new information sent to the reporting contact. This policy applies to project spaces and people representing the project.
